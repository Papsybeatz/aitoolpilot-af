Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use AIToolPilot:

  • Email address — when you subscribe to our newsletter
  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, referral source (via analytics)
  • Click data — which affiliate links you click (anonymized)

We do not collect payment information. We do not sell your personal data.

How We Use Your Information

  • To send the weekly newsletter you subscribed to
  • To understand which content is most useful to our readers
  • To improve the site and our tool recommendations

Cookies and Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google LLC, to understand how visitors use this site. It collects information such as the pages you visit, how long you stay, the site that referred you, your approximate location (city level), and your device, browser, and operating system.

Google Analytics sets first-party cookies named _ga and _ga_* to distinguish unique visitors. These cookies can persist for up to two years. Google Analytics does not log or store your full IP address.

How to opt out: you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or use a browser or extension that blocks tracking scripts. Blocking analytics does not affect your ability to use the site.

For details on how Google handles data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites that use our services.

We also record clicks on affiliate links as analytics events (as described above) and store them temporarily in your browser's session storage. This data is used only by us and our analytics provider, and is not sold or shared further.

Data Processors and Service Providers

We use the following third-party services to operate this site. Each processes data on our behalf and is bound by its own privacy policy:

  • Kit (ConvertKit LLC) — email marketing. Stores your email address and subscription details when you join our newsletter, and delivers our emails. Privacy policy: kit.com/privacy
  • Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC) — website analytics. Collects usage data and sets analytics cookies as described above. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Affiliate networks and partners (including Impact.com and Pabbly) — track clicks on affiliate links so that commissions can be attributed to us. These networks may set their own cookies when you click an affiliate link. Their privacy policies apply to that processing.

Third-Party Links

This site contains affiliate links to third-party products and services. When you click these links, you are subject to the privacy policies of those third-party sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Newsletter

If you subscribe to our newsletter, your email address is stored with Kit (ConvertKit), our email service provider, and is used only to send you the emails you requested. Our newsletter uses double opt-in, which means you must confirm your subscription by email before you are added to the list.

You can unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email we send, or by contacting us. We do not sell or rent your email address.

Data Retention

We retain newsletter subscriber data until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is retained for 26 months.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
