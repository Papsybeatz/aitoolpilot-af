Pabbly Connect Review (2026)

The budget Zapier alternative — 2,000+ integrations and no task charges for triggers or internal steps

⭐ 4.4 / 5 Free plan · Paid from $19/month · Lifetime from $349 Best for: Budget-conscious small businesses, Zapier refugees
Try Pabbly Connect Free Plan · No Card

Overview

Pabbly Connect is a no-code automation platform that connects 2,000+ apps and runs multi-step workflows on a trigger. It's the tool most people find when they go looking for a cheaper Zapier — and it earns that reputation: you get a free tier that needs no credit card, plans that cost a fraction of Zapier's, and a one-time lifetime option.

Its real differentiator is how it counts usage. Triggers and internal steps — filters, routers, formatters — don't consume tasks. Only actions performed in an external app count. On Zapier, filters and paths can eat into your allowance. At volume, that difference alone can cut your bill dramatically.

Key Features

  • 2,000+ Integrations: Broad coverage of the apps small businesses actually use
  • Multi-Step Workflows: Chain triggers, filters, routers, and formatters without code
  • No Task Charges for Internal Steps: Filters, routers, and formatters are free — only external actions count
  • Instant Webhooks: Real-time triggers for custom and unsupported apps
  • Scheduled & Instant Triggers: Run workflows on a schedule or the moment something happens
  • Auto Re-execution: Retry failed or skipped steps automatically
  • Lifetime Option: Pay once for a fixed monthly task allowance, with no recurring bill

Who Is It For?

  • Small businesses automating on a tight budget
  • Anyone who has hit Zapier's task limits or pricing ceiling
  • Operations teams running repetitive multi-app workflows
  • Agencies standardising automation across client accounts
  • Founders who prefer paying once over a permanent subscription

Pabbly Connect vs Zapier vs Make

  • Price: Pabbly is the cheapest per task at volume — and the only one with a lifetime option
  • Task counting: Pabbly doesn't charge for triggers or internal steps; Zapier charges for filters and paths
  • Polish: Zapier is the easiest to start with; Pabbly is functional but less refined
  • Complexity: Make is strongest for intricate branching logic; Pabbly handles standard multi-step workflows well
  • Integrations: Zapier has the deepest catalogue; Pabbly's 2,000+ covers most small-business needs
  • Verdict: Zapier for simplicity, Make for complex logic, Pabbly for value — especially at high volume
