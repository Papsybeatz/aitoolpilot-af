Pabbly Connect vs Make

Both undercut Zapier on price. Which one is the smarter pick for your business?

Make and Pabbly Connect are the two tools most people land on after deciding Zapier is too expensive. They solve the same problem from opposite directions: Make gives you more power for less money, while Pabbly gives you more volume for less money. Here's how to choose.

Our recommendation

Choose Pabbly Connect if:

  • Your workflows are straightforward but high-volume
  • You want predictable costs and a lifetime option
  • You'd rather not spend a weekend learning a visual builder
  • You rely heavily on filters, routers, or formatting steps

Choose Make if:

  • You need genuinely complex, branching scenarios
  • You're comfortable with a visual, flowchart-style editor
  • You want the lowest entry price and a generous free tier
  • You need advanced data transformation and error handling

The bottom line: Make is the better tool for complex logic; Pabbly is the better deal for straightforward volume. If you're automating standard business processes and watching costs, Pabbly wins. If your workflows look like flowcharts, Make wins.

Frequently asked questions

Is Pabbly Connect or Make better for beginners?

Pabbly is generally easier to start with because its builder is simpler and more linear. Make rewards you with more power once you learn it, but the learning curve is real.

Which is cheaper, Pabbly Connect or Make?

Make's paid entry plan is cheaper at $9/month. But Pabbly doesn't charge tasks for triggers or internal steps, so at real volume Pabbly often ends up cheaper — and it has a one-time lifetime option Make doesn't offer.

Does Make have a lifetime plan?

No. Make is subscription-only. Pabbly Connect sells one-time lifetime plans starting at $349 for 3,000 tasks per month.
