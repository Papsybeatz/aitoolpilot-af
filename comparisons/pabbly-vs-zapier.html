Our recommendation

Choose Zapier if:

  • You need a niche app that only Zapier supports
  • You want the simplest possible setup and don't mind paying for it
  • You're automating a handful of low-volume workflows

Choose Pabbly Connect if:

  • Your task volume is growing and your Zapier bill is climbing with it
  • You use a lot of filters, routers, or formatting steps — Pabbly doesn't charge for those
  • You'd rather pay once than keep a subscription running
  • You're comfortable with a less polished interface in exchange for real savings

The bottom line: Zapier wins on polish and app coverage. Pabbly wins on cost — often by a wide margin once you count internal steps. For a small business running real volume, Pabbly is the better financial decision.

Frequently asked questions

Is Pabbly Connect a good Zapier alternative?

Yes. It covers 2,000+ integrations and doesn't charge tasks for triggers or internal steps, which usually makes it much cheaper than Zapier at the same volume. The trade-off is a less polished interface and a smaller app catalogue.

Does Pabbly Connect have a free plan?

Yes — 100 tasks per month with no credit card required, limited to two workflows of up to two steps each. It's enough to test the platform properly before paying.

Is Pabbly cheaper than Zapier?

At comparable volume, almost always. Pabbly's Standard plan gives 10,000 tasks for $19/month ($16/month billed yearly), and internal steps don't consume tasks. Pabbly also sells one-time lifetime plans from $349.

Can Pabbly Connect fully replace Zapier?

For most small-business workflows, yes. Zapier still has the larger integration catalogue, so check that every app you depend on is supported before you switch. Migration itself is straightforward.
