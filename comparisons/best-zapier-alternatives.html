7 Best Zapier Alternatives for Small Business (2026)

Ranked by value, power and ease of use — so you can pick the right one in five minutes.

Zapier is excellent, but it's also the most expensive way to automate a small business. If your bill keeps climbing, or you've hit your task limit and can't justify the next tier, one of these will do the job for less.

Quick picks

  • Best overall value: Pabbly Connect — 2,000+ apps, no task charges for internal steps, lifetime plans
  • Best for complex workflows: Make — visual builder with advanced logic
  • Best for developers: n8n — open-source and self-hostable
  • Best free tier: Make — 1,000 operations per month
  • Simplest to set up: Integrately — ready-made one-click automations

1. Pabbly Connect — best overall value

Pabbly is the tool most people switch to when they get tired of paying Zapier by the task. It covers 2,000+ integrations, and — crucially — triggers and internal steps like filters, routers and formatters don't count against your task allowance. Only actions in external apps do.

It also has a genuine free plan (100 tasks, no card), and it's the only major platform on this list that sells one-time lifetime plans.

Pros

  • Cheapest at real volume
  • Internal steps don't consume tasks
  • Free plan, no credit card
  • Lifetime options from $349

Cons

  • Less polished interface
  • No team members on monthly plans
  • Smaller catalogue than Zapier

2. Make — best for complex workflows

Make (formerly Integromat) gives you a visual, flowchart-style builder with real branching, iterators and error handling. Its free tier is the most generous here at 1,000 operations per month, and paid plans start at $9/month. The trade-off is a steeper learning curve.

Pros

  • Most powerful visual builder
  • Largest free tier
  • Cheapest paid entry at $9/mo

Cons

  • Steep learning curve
  • Operations counting can be confusing

3. n8n — best for developers

n8n is open-source and can be self-hosted for free, which makes it the darling of technical teams who don't want per-task pricing at all. It's powerful and extensible, but you'll be comfortable with nodes, APIs and possibly servers. For non-technical small businesses it's usually more than you need.

4. Integrately — best for ready-made automations

Integrately's pitch is "1-click automations" — a library of pre-built workflows you can switch on without building anything. Good if you want results fast and don't care about designing custom logic.

5. IFTTT — best for simple, free automations

IFTTT is consumer-grade: simple if-this-then-that applets across hundreds of services, with a free tier. It's great for personal or very light business automations, but it isn't built for multi-step business workflows.

6. Microsoft Power Automate — best if you're in Microsoft 365

If your business already runs on Microsoft 365, Power Automate is often included in your plan and integrates deeply with Outlook, Teams, SharePoint and Excel. The catch is that it's most valuable inside the Microsoft ecosystem.

7. Zapier — the baseline

Worth naming, because it's still the best at what it does: 5,000+ apps, the most polished interface, and the least chance of hitting an integration that doesn't exist. If simplicity matters more than cost, stay.

How to choose in 30 seconds: If cost is your problem, start with Pabbly Connect. If complexity is your problem, use Make. If you're a developer who wants to self-host, use n8n. If nothing is wrong and you just want the safest option, stay on Zapier.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free Zapier alternative?

Make has the most generous free tier with 1,000 operations per month. Pabbly Connect and Zapier both offer 100 tasks free. IFTTT is free and easiest for simple consumer-style automations.

What is the cheapest Zapier alternative for high volume?

Pabbly Connect is usually cheapest at high volume, because triggers and internal steps like filters and routers don't count as tasks. It also offers one-time lifetime plans starting at $349.

Is there a Zapier alternative with a lifetime deal?

Yes — Pabbly Connect sells one-time plans (Standard Lifetime $349 for 3,000 tasks/month, Ultimate $799 for 10,000, Ultimate Plus $1,298 for 20,000). Make and Zapier are subscription-only.
