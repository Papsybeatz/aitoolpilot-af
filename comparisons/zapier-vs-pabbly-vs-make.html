The verdict

  • Pick Zapier if you want zero friction and need a niche app nobody else supports. You'll pay for it.
  • Pick Pabbly Connect if cost is the deciding factor and your workflows are standard. The task-counting model is the real advantage.
  • Pick Make if your automation looks like a flowchart and you're willing to learn the builder.

Most small businesses should start with Pabbly Connect. It's the cheapest at real volume, the free plan needs no card, and it's the only one of the three you can buy outright instead of renting forever.

Frequently asked questions

Which is best: Zapier, Pabbly or Make?

It depends on your constraint. Zapier is best for beginners and app coverage. Pabbly is best for cost at volume and offers lifetime plans. Make is best for complex logic and has the most generous free tier.

Which automation tool is cheapest for a small business?

Make has the cheapest paid entry ($9/month) and the biggest free tier (1,000 operations). Pabbly usually wins at high volume because triggers and internal steps don't count as tasks.

Can I switch between them later?

Yes. Workflows are rebuildable in an afternoon for most small businesses. Start with the free plans and only commit once you know your real task volume.
